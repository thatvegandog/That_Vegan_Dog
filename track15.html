OTHER SIDE

Another loss, but I’m sure it ain’t the last
Do they think the good is there to hide the past
Every day I try to look right in the eyes
Why don’t I see what lies behind the other side

Why don’t I care about the ones who seem so blessed
I neglect the truth and leave out all the rest
Another one is gone beyond and all I feel
Remorse, regret, what isn’t meant to be

My pride resides and it won’t show what’s true
Call out the world but I think I’m ad cruel
My hands are just too red to call for kind
Promise I’ll look for the other side

In a haze that forms and takes away a smile
Yet again, when I thought everything was fine
This time I know that I’m the one at fault
A cloud of guilt that builds beside the storm

Why am I the one I hated most
Will I decide to help the ones at low
Remembering that it’s the final time
But I cross a line behind the other side
