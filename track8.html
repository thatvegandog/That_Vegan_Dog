SUFFER

Trouble, turning its back right upon me
Where do I go when everyone wants to stop me
Drag by a chain, all these thoughts have me pale
But I’m sure everytime this fucked fate has fought me

Sorry, this was far from the cry
Why’d I deny when I didn’t even try
Know it seems ugly when you are in depths of shame
But I lie because I know I don’t want to fail

Everywhere that I look, nothing hooks but I see
I’m down in the dark, everyone’s up and free
All I hear when it breaks, echoes of a scream
It’s the same all around, nothing left for me to be

Snow closes in but it isn’t even over
No one sees the end but it views me as closer
Broken shovel, hands, laughs, just like the others
No going back, we’ll be this forever
We’ll suffer
